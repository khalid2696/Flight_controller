This paper deals with simulation of P^2 controller on Quaternion based approach. 
This is nearly similar to our approach with small changes.
Paper only deals with simulation
Algo takes both torque and Velocity (direction, Magnitude) into account and control accordingly using the weights given.

DIFFERENCES:
1)P^2 controller vs regular PID
2)Implementation transformations are not given
3)Integral term is not there. So it will not stabilize

POSSIBLE MISTAKE:
It is stated that integral is not needed due to some non linear dynamics. This wont be true. W term will act like derivative and q term will act for the proportional part
